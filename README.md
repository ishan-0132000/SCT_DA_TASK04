# Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis | Business Insights (EDA)

## Project Overview

Marketing performance is not just about generating clicks or impressions. Businesses need to understand which channels deliver better returns, where potential customers drop off, and how marketing budgets can be allocated more effectively.

This project explores a simulated marketing campaign dataset using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to evaluate campaign performance, understand the conversion funnel, compare marketing channels and develop data-driven business recommendations.

The primary objective is to answer **why certain marketing channels perform differently and what businesses should consider before reallocating their marketing budget.**

## Business Objectives

- Understand overall marketing campaign performance.
- Clean and prepare campaign data for analysis.
- Analyze the marketing conversion funnel.
- Compare the performance of different marketing channels.
- Evaluate Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) and Cost Per Acquisition (CPA).
- Identify opportunities for improving marketing efficiency.
- Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

## Key Business Findings

1. **Channel Performance:** Email recorded the highest modeled ROAS, while Display recorded the lowest in the available summary.

2. **Conversion Funnel:** CTR and click-to-conversion rate provide two useful indicators for understanding campaign engagement and conversion efficiency.

3. **Marketing Efficiency:** CPA helps evaluate the media cost associated with generating conversions.

4. **Budget Decisions:** Historical ROAS alone should not determine budget allocation. Businesses should also evaluate contribution margin, full operating costs and incremental conversions.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate unusually high or low channel returns before changing budgets.
- Improve audience targeting and creative relevance.
- Optimize landing pages to improve conversion rates.
- Conduct controlled experiments to measure incremental campaign impact.
- Evaluate customer lifetime value and contribution margin alongside ROAS.
- Allocate additional budget only when incremental returns justify the investment.

## Important Limitations

The dataset is simulated for analytical practice. The available project summary does not contain the original row-level campaign data needed to independently reproduce every metric.

The analysis also lacks gross margin, refunds, customer lifetime value, audience overlap, attribution windows and complete channel operating costs.

Therefore, attributed ROAS should not be interpreted as proof of incremental revenue or profitability.
